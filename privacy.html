Privacy Policy

Pawty Clash · Effective 23 November 2026

Short version: we (ParadigX) collect no personal data ourselves — no accounts, no names, no contacts. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which processes limited device and usage data to serve and measure non-personalised, G-rated ads. Buying "Unlock all" removes all ads.

What we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal data. Pawty Clash has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics of our own.

What Google AdMob collects (free version)

To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect: device identifiers (not the advertising identifier used for tracking, as we never request tracking permission), approximate location derived from the IP address, product interaction and advertising data (for example which ad was shown or tapped), and diagnostics. This data is not linked to your identity by us and is not used to track you across other companies' apps.

Ads (free version only)

The free version shows short ads between rounds and optional "watch an ad" rewards, served by Google AdMob. Every ad request is tagged as child-directed, limited to G-rated (all ages) content and set to non-personalised ads. We do not ask for permission to track you, and ads are not targeted using your activity in other apps. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical data such as the device type, operating system, approximate location from the IP address and ad interactions. See Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Ads never show while you are offline.

What stays on your device

Your settings (language, sound, vibration), chosen pets and their levels are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The optional "Unlock all" purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. It also removes all ads.

Children

Pawty Clash is a family game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; every ad request is tagged child-directed and limited to G-rated content, and purchases go through Apple's standard confirmation (Ask to Buy and Screen Time work as usual).

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: github.com/limutong/paw-clash/issues
