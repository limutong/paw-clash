Privacy Policy

Paw Clash · Effective 22 November 2026

Short version: Paw Clash does not collect any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. Paw Clash has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party advertising SDKs. We do not collect names, contacts, location, device identifiers or usage data.

What stays on your device

Your settings (language, sound, vibration), chosen pets and their levels are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The optional "Unlock all" purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions.

Children

Paw Clash is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children. Purchases go through Apple's standard confirmation, and parents can use Ask to Buy and Screen Time.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: github.com/limutong/paw-clash/issues
